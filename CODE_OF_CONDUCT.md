# OmniCOVAS community conduct

OmniCOVAS is a place for Elite Dangerous Commanders to help one another,
report problems, share ideas, and improve the companion experience.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing about a feature,
  game mechanic, or development decision.
- Welcome new and experienced Commanders alike. Explain acronyms or technical
  steps when doing so will help.
- Share enough detail to make feedback useful, while protecting your own and
  others' privacy. Never post passwords, tokens, private messages, unredacted
  game journals, or other people's personal data.
- Keep reports and discussions relevant; avoid spam, promotion, harassment,
  discriminatory abuse, threats, impersonation, and deliberate disruption.
- Respect licensing, permissions, third-party services, and Elite Dangerous's
  rules. Do not solicit gameplay botting, cheating, or security bypasses.

## Moderation

The maintainer may edit labels, move discussions, hide harmful content, lock
threads, or restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe.
Feedback and disagreement are welcome; abuse is not.

To privately report a conduct concern, email **rocketsprojects.git@gmail.com**
with the subject `OmniCOVAS community conduct`. Include links and relevant
context, but avoid forwarding additional private information unnecessarily.
Reports are reviewed as volunteer capacity allows; confidentiality will be
respected where practical but cannot be absolutely guaranteed.

For vulnerabilities, use the separate [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).
For the terms applying to code and feedback, see [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md)
and [License](LICENSE.md).
